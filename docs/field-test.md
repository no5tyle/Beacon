# Field test

Phase 5: running a real unit night on the new app at https://no5tyle.github.io/Beacon/ instead of the current one.
Everything here talks to real Terrain and changes real records, so use statements the youth have actually done.
Note anything that differs from the current app, with the phone and browser, in an issue on this repository.

## Before the night (at home, about 15 minutes)

1. **Log in on your phone** with the password manager's autofill. It should log in by itself once the fields are filled, on iOS and on Android if you have one. Tap the version line at the bottom if it doesn't, and copy what it shows into the issue.
2. **Install it**: follow the install hint on Insights (Share, then Add to Home Screen on iPhone). Open it from the Home Screen and check nothing sits under the notch, the rounded corners or the home indicator.
3. **Insights**: check the tiles, the OAS gaps and the priority youth look the same as in the current app for the same unit. Pull down from the top to refresh.
4. **Progress**: open two or three youth and compare their details with the current app, including a youth who came up from Joeys or Cubs. Pull down on the list to refresh.
5. **Beacon Reference**: on the Reference page, tap "Open as the Beacon Reference app", add it to the Home Screen, open it, then use "Save everything for offline" in its menu. Turn on flight mode and check it still opens and searches. Share a statement and open the link on another phone.

## On the night

Use only the new app. If you have to go back to the current app for anything, note what and why; the night doesn't count.

1. **Sign off a stage for several youth** with Record: pick statements, filter by patrol, choose youth, save. Check the results list, then find one of the youth in Terrain and check the statements, verifier and date are there.
2. **Submit for review**: when a youth's stage is complete, use "Submit for review" on the results, and check the stage shows as waiting for Unit Council review in Terrain.
3. **Record one youth's statements** from their Progress details ("Record statements" next to a stage), and submit one stage that way.
4. **A Joey or Cub record**: if your group has a Joey or Cub unit, save at least one statement for one of them, ideally a youth whose records were ticked through events.
5. **Stage 6 and above**: open a Stage 6 stage and check only the logbook can be ticked.

## After the night

- Tick the remaining items on the [parity checklist](plan.md#parity-checklist).
- Phase 5 is done once a full unit night has run on the new app with no fallback to the current one and every item is ticked. Then it's Phase 6, the switch over.
